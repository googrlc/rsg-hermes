# Communication Templates

Starting-point templates for client communication. Always customize
with the client's name, specifics, and a warm tone. Gretchen reviews
and sends — these are drafts, not auto-sends.

## When to use

- "Draft a client reply."
- "Draft a response asking for missing info."
- "Draft a welcome email."
- "Draft a follow-up message."
- Any time you need a warm, professional starting point for a client
  message.

## General service request response

```
Hi [Client],

Thanks for reaching out. I have your request about [topic].

Here is what I will do:
1. [First action]
2. [Second action]

I will follow up with you by [date/time]. If you need anything sooner,
just let me know.

Thanks,
Gretchen
```

## Missing information request

forward:

1. [Missing item 1]
2. [Missing item 2]
3. [Missing item 3]

Once I have these, I can get this wrapped up for you quickly.

Thanks,
Gretchen
```

## Renewal — no increase

```
Hi [Client],

Your [Line of Business] policy with [Carrier] is renewing on [Date].
The premium is [Amount], the same as last year.

No action needed — the policy will renew automatically. Let me know
if you have any questions.

Thanks,
Gretchen
```

## Renewal — small increase (under 10%)

```
Hi [Client],

Your [Line of Business] policy with [Carrier] is renewing on [Date].
The new premium is [Amount], up from [Old Amount] — about [X]% more.

This is a normal market adjustment. I have reviewed the coverage and
everything looks good. If you would like to talk about options to bring
it down, I am happy to look.

Thanks,
Gretchen
```

## Renewal — larger increase (10%+)

```
Hi [Client],

Your [Line of Business] policy with [Carrier] is renewing on [Date].
The new premium is [Amount], up from [Old Amount] — about [X]% more.

I want to look at this together. Here is what I recommend:
1. I will review the policy for any discounts or adjustments.
2. I will explain what is driving the increase.
3. If you would like, I can shop this with other carriers to compare.

Would you like me to go ahead and review, or do you want to look at the
renewal first?

Thanks,
Gretchen
```

## COI — missing information

```
Hi [Client],

I can get that certificate of insurance processed for you. To make sure
it is accurate, I need a few details:

1. Certificate holder name and address
2. Any special wording required (additional insured, waiver of
   subrogation, primary and noncontributory)
3. The job or project name
4. When you need it by

Once I have that, I will get it turned around for you.

Thanks,
Gretchen
```

Gretchen
```

## Follow-up — no response

```
Hi [Client],

Just checking in on the information needed to continue your insurance

next step.

Is there anything I can help with?

Thanks,
Gretchen
```

## Tone guidelines

- Always warm and professional — never stiff or corporate.
- Use the client's first name unless they prefer otherwise.
- Keep it short — one screen, not a letter.
- Lead with the answer or the action, not the background.
- If asking for something, make the list short and specific.
- Close with an offer to help — "let me know" or "call me if you need
  anything."
